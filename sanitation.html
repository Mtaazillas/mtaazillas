Taking Back

A clean environment isn't a luxury, it’s a standard of living. We organize the youth to sweep, clear, and reclaim Nairobi’s neighborhoods from illegal dumpsites.

Phase 01

Community Mobilization

Before we lift a single piece of trash, we engage the residents. Our youth leaders walk door-to-door, explaining the impact of illegal dumping and recruiting locals to join the upcoming drive. Ownership starts with participation.

Phase 02

The Mega Cleanup

Armed with shovels, rakes, masks, and wheelbarrows, our volunteer army descends on targeted black spots. We clear choked drainage systems to prevent flooding and physically dismantle illegal dumpsites that have plagued corners for years.

Phase 03

Infrastructure & Maintenance

Cleaning isn't enough; we have to maintain it. Mtaazillas installs welded public trash bins in high-traffic areas and partners with local county trucks to ensure scheduled pickups, preventing the trash from piling up again.

"When you change what a street looks like, you change how the people living on it feel about themselves. We are restoring dignity."

— Mtaazillas Sanitation Team
