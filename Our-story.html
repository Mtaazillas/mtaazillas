How It Began

We Are Mtaazillas

It started as an idea — a simple question of how we could transform the way we live, beginning with cleaning up our own streets and teaching workshops to those around us.

By mid-2025, that idea had grown into something real: a project, tested and carried out on the ground in Viwandani.

By mid-2026, we realized this couldn't stay a single project — it needed to become something bigger. We brought together creative minds ready to build collective impact, and Mtaazillas stopped being a project. It became a movement.

Our Journey

From One Table To A Movement

The Idea
A conversation that became a calling

A small gathering — twelve people, one table, and a shared frustration about challenges no one else seemed to be addressing. That evening, something shifted.

What we're building toward

Registration, growth, sustainable programmes, and a community that trusts us. We're in the early days — and we intend to make them count.

Our Mission

To Mobilise & Empower

To empower the youth and residents of Viwandani and Nairobi's underserved neighbourhoods to transform their own communities — through street sanitation, waste-to-value enterprise, health outreach, and grassroots leadership — building solutions that belong to the people who live them.

Our Vision

A Thriving, Dignified Nairobi

A Nairobi where every neighbourhood is clean, healthy, and self-driven — where young people turn waste into opportunity, community members lead their own change, and no problem closest to home is left for someone else to solve.

Where We're Headed

The Impact We're Working Toward

We're one month in. Here's the change we're building toward across our five pillars.

Health

Connecting youth to clinics, screenings & wellness education in every neighbourhood we reach.

Sanitation

Cleaner streets and dignified, accessible waste collection for informal settlements.

Youth Empowerment

Mentorship, skills training & leadership pathways for young people ready to lead.

Waste To Value

Turning waste into income — upcycling and recycling initiatives owned by the community.

Environment

Tree-planting and clean-ups that help Nairobi's neighbourhoods breathe easier.

Looking Ahead

What We're Bringing To Nairobi Next

We're early — and intentional about it. Here's what we're working toward over the next chapter.

  • Formal registration & transparent governance structures
  • Scaling our six pillars into more Nairobi neighbourhoods
  • Partnerships with local businesses, donors & institutions
  • Growing from 10 founders into a movement of hundreds
