Our Network

Our Partners

Impact is never a solo effort. Meet the organizations, businesses, and civic bodies that amplify our work across Nairobi.

Health & Medical Partners

KRC

Kenya Red Cross

Provides emergency response training, first aid certification, and medical personnel for our quarterly free health camps.

NHC

Nairobi Health Clinics

A coalition of local dispensaries that offer subsidized follow-up treatments for patients identified during our community screenings.

AMF

Afya Mtaani Foundation

Partners with our youth advocates to deliver reproductive health education and distribute sanitary products in schools.

Environmental & Sanitation

NEMA

NEMA Kenya

Provides regulatory guidance, seedlings for urban greening, and waste disposal logistics for massive river cleanups.

GWC

Green Waste Collective

Purchases the sorted recyclable plastics collected by our youth, converting our sanitation efforts into a direct economic stream.

TFK

Trees For Kenya

Co-sponsors our "One Youth, One Tree" initiative, providing indigenous seedlings and environmental maintenance training.
